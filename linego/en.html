Introduction

Line Go (「一线牵」) is a line-drawing puzzle game: tap adjacent dots to connect them and traverse every line in a level to clear it. The app is developed and operated by an independent developer, Marvin ("we", "us", "our"), and is currently distributed as a WeChat Mini Game and an Android application.

This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and store it, with whom we share it, and what rights you have. We process personal information in accordance with the principles of lawfulness, purpose limitation, minimum necessity, transparency and accountability.

Key points in three lines No account is required to play, and your game progress is stored on your device by default.
We do not request location permission and do not collect your precise location, contacts, SMS, photos, camera or microphone content.
The app contains advertising and analytics services, which collect device identifiers as necessary; you can opt out of personalized ads in your system settings.

Please read this policy carefully before using the app. By starting to use the app you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy. Where sensitive personal information or separate consent is required, we will highlight the relevant terms and obtain your explicit consent before processing.

5. How we use information

  • Core functionality: saving and restoring progress, leaderboards, hints and scores.
  • Safety and fairness: detecting abnormal devices and cheating, preventing leaderboard abuse, maintaining stability.
  • Product improvement: analyzing level completion and drop-off points to tune difficulty, UI and performance.
  • Advertising: displaying ads, measuring impressions and clicks, evaluating campaigns and capping frequency.
  • Support: responding to your inquiries, complaints and rights requests.
  • Legal obligations: cooperating with regulators and lawful requests from authorities.

We do not use your information for unrelated purposes, for automated decisions that produce legal effects, or for sale or rental to any third party.

8. Security

  • Data in transit is protected with HTTPS / TLS encryption.
  • We apply the minimum necessity principle to all data access.
  • Server-side access is controlled by account and permission management with audit logs.
  • We use only officially released, up-to-date SDK versions and conduct regular security reviews.
  • Personnel with data access are bound by confidentiality obligations and receive privacy training.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will activate our incident response plan, take remedial measures, and notify you of the incident, its likely impact and the steps you can take, and report to regulators as required by law.

10. Children

Line Go is a casual puzzle game suitable for general audiences, but we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.

  • If you are under 14, please read this policy with a parent or guardian and use the app only with their consent.
  • If you are a minor, please use the app under the guidance of a guardian.
  • Where we process a child's personal information with a guardian's separate consent, we apply stricter safeguards and do not serve personalized advertising based on it.
  • Guardians who believe we have collected a child's information without consent may contact us and we will delete it promptly after verification.

11. Personalized advertising and opt-out

Ads shown in the app may be personalized by third-party ad platforms (such as Google AdMob) based on device identifiers and approximate location. You can opt out:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → delete advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track".
  • WeChat Mini Game: WeChat → Me → Settings → Personal Information & Permissions → Personalized Ads → turn off.
  • Google account: manage settings at adssettings.google.com.

After opting out you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant. Opting out does not affect the basic processing required to serve and measure ads.

12. Local storage and similar technologies

The app does not use browser cookies for cross-site tracking. We use local storage on your device to save progress and speed up loading: save files (level progress, settings, WeChat OpenID), cache files (images, audio, level configuration), and SDK-generated local identifiers. You can delete this data by clearing app data or uninstalling; unsynced progress cannot be recovered afterwards.

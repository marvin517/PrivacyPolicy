Introduction

Crazy Brick (the "App") is a brick-breaker: slide the paddle, launch, and the ball automatically aims for the gaps at the bottom of the map, then melts the wall from the inside out. Power-ups multiply your balls (up to 350 on screen), with a 120-level campaign and an endless mode. The App is developed and operated by the independent developer Marvin ("we", "us") and is currently offered as an Android app (distributed via Google Play) and a web build (running in the browser via itch.io and similar platforms), in Simplified Chinese and English.

We take the protection of your personal information seriously. This policy explains what we collect, why, how we use and store it, with whom we share it, and what rights you have.

The policy in three lines ① No account is needed to play. Level progress, stars and settings stay on your device.
② We do not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS or call-log permissions, and we do not collect precise location or biometric data.
③ The Android build integrates Google AdMob rewarded video ads (currently used only for "watch an ad to revive" after a failed level). There are no in-app purchases and no third-party analytics. The web build contains no ads at all.

1. Scope of this policy

1.1 Products covered: this policy covers the Android version of Crazy Brick (distributed via Google Play) and the web version (running in the browser via itch.io and similar platforms). Features differ between channels: ads exist only in the Android build; the web build is fully playable offline and contains no advertising. The version you actually use governs.

1.2 Not covered: the distribution platforms (such as itch.io) and system/store services (such as Google Play) may use cookies, device identifiers and analytics of their own, governed by their own privacy policies. Ads shown inside the App and third-party pages they link to are provided by the respective providers, who are solely responsible for their information practices.

1.3 Data controller: Marvin (independent developer). Contact details are in section 14.

5. How we use information

  • Core functionality: saving and restoring level progress, stars, endless-mode records and settings;
  • Config and content updates: fetching remote config and hot-update resources from the CDN to tune level parameters and fix issues;
  • Ads and rewards (Android only): showing rewarded videos and granting the revive reward after a completed view, with frequency capping;
  • Product improvement: difficulty and balance tuning via remote config (not user-level statistics);

  • Legal obligations: cooperating with regulators and competent authorities.

We will never use your personal information for unrelated purposes, for automated decisions with legal effect on you, or sell or rent it to anyone.

Your game progress and settings are stored locally on your device (browser local storage for the web build, the app's private directory on Android) and are never uploaded to our servers. The App integrates no third-party analytics, so there is no analytics database. Ad-related data is stored on Google's (AdMob) servers.

If the App ceases operation, we will notify you in advance via in-game notices and the store page, and delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period unless the law requires otherwise.

7. Sharing, transfer and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information; the processing list is in section 4. Under law and national standards we may share, transfer or disclose personal information without prior consent when it is:

Despite these measures, no internet environment is absolutely secure. If a personal-information incident unfortunately occurs, we will activate our contingency plan, take remedial action, notify you of the incident, its possible impact and suggested precautions via in-app notices or announcements, and report to regulators as required by law.

11. Ads, consent management (UMP) and opt-out

The Android build currently shows only Google AdMob rewarded video ads, used exclusively for "watch an ad to revive" after a failed level — you can always skip the ad and simply retry, and no feature is locked behind ads. The web build contains no advertising.

11.1 Consent management for EEA / UK users

On first launch in the EEA / UK, the Android build shows a Google consent form (UMP) where you choose:

  • Accept personalized ads: ads are targeted using your advertising ID and coarse location for higher relevance;
  • Non-personalized ads only: rewarded videos still work for revive, with lower relevance;
  • Reject all: ad revive becomes unavailable; nothing else is affected.

You can bring the form up again any time from system settings to change your choice.

11.2 Turning off personalization

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization";
  • No ad requests at all: play offline (the game is fully playable offline), or simply never use ad revive.

After turning off personalization you may still see non-personalized ads, possibly more frequently; nothing outside ad revive is affected.

12. Local storage and similar technologies

The App does not use browser cookies for cross-site tracking. To store progress and speed up loading we use local storage on your device, including:

  • Local saves: campaign level progress and stars, endless-mode wave records, theme and language choices, sound and ball-collision toggles and other settings;
  • Cache: remote config and hot-update resources, to avoid repeated downloads (cached or built-in content is used offline).

You can delete all of the above by clearing app or browser data. Game progress cannot be recovered afterwards.

Introduction

Rebirth (Chinese title 重生, hereinafter "the App") is a data-driven life simulation game: you press "Be born", the game draws your country of birth, sex, urban or rural setting, family and parents from real birth statistics, and then you live that life one year at a time — event probabilities come from published statistical data. The App is independently developed and operated by Marvin ("we", "us"). It is offered as an Android app (distributed through Google Play, itch.io and similar channels) and as a web build running in the browser (for example on itch.io), in Simplified Chinese and English.

We understand how important personal information is to you and will do our best to keep it safe and reliable. This policy explains what we collect, why, how we use and retain it, who we share it with, and what rights you have. We process personal information in line with the principles of accountability, clear purpose, consent, data minimisation, security, user participation and transparency.

The policy in three sentences ① No account is required — each life's save file stays on your device;
② We do not collect your name, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, and we do not read or upload your location;
③ We use anonymous gameplay analytics (GameAnalytics) to understand progression and drop-off points, and the Android build bundles Google AdMob ad capability (ad placements are currently switched off in the live configuration).

Please read this policy carefully before using the App. By starting to use the App you confirm that you have read and accepted this policy. Where a clause involves sensitive personal information or requires separate consent, we will highlight it and only process that information after obtaining your explicit consent.

1. Scope of this policy

1.1 Products covered: this policy applies to the Android version of Rebirth (distributed via Google Play, itch.io and similar channels) and to the web version (running in the browser via itch.io and similar platforms). Features may differ between channels — for example, ads and certain platform capabilities are only available in the Android version.

1.2 Not covered: the distribution platforms that host the App (such as itch.io) and the operating-system and store services on Android (such as Google Play) may use cookies, device identifiers and analytics of their own; their processing is governed by their own privacy policies and is not covered by this policy. Third-party ads, cross-promotion entries and external pages linked from the App are provided by those third parties, who are independently responsible for their own data protection. We recommend reading their privacy policies before submitting any information to them.

1.3 Data controller: Marvin (independent developer). Contact details are in section 14.

So that analytics and (if enabled) advertising work, our analytics and advertising providers may return aggregated or anonymous result data to us (for example total event counts or ad impression and click figures — never your name or phone number). 2.4 What we never collect

We do not collect: your name, phone number or email address (other than what you send us voluntarily), government ID numbers, bank card or payment account details, contacts, SMS or call logs, photo library or file contents, microphone recordings, camera images, biometric data such as face, fingerprint or voiceprints, or your precise location.

The App does not read device location and does not upload location data to any server. If a future feature genuinely needs such information, we will explain the purpose, method and scope separately and obtain your separate consent under applicable law before any processing begins.

5. How we use information

We use your information only for the purposes below, and will seek your consent again before using it beyond that scope:

  • Providing core features: saving and restoring each life's progress, life archive and interface preferences;
  • Safety and stability: detecting abnormal launches and configuration failures, fixing crashes and compatibility issues;
  • Improving the product: analysing progression and drop-off, calibrating event probabilities, the mortality model and interface performance;
  • Advertising and measurement (only when ads are enabled): displaying ads, capping frequency, counting impressions and clicks and settling billing;
  • Customer support: answering your questions, complaints and rights requests;
  • Legal obligations: cooperating with regulatory reviews and lawful requests from judicial or administrative authorities.

We do not use your personal information for purposes unrelated to the above, for automated decisions that produce legal effects, or for sale or rental to any third party.

6. Storage and cross-border transfer

6.1 Where data lives

Your game progress, life archive and settings are stored locally on your device (the app's private directory on Android; browser local storage on web) and are not uploaded to our servers. We operate no account server and keep no copy of your saves. Anonymous analytics data is stored on GameAnalytics' servers; advertising data (when ads are on) is stored on Google's servers.

Other than as described above, we do not provide your personal information to overseas recipients. If new cross-border processing scenarios arise, we will inform you, carry out a personal information protection impact assessment, and obtain your separate consent as required by law.

If the App is discontinued, we will notify you in advance through in-app announcements and store listings, and delete or anonymise your personal information within a reasonable period, unless laws require otherwise.

8. Security

We apply industry-standard safeguards to protect your information:

  • network traffic is encrypted with HTTPS / TLS to prevent interception or tampering in transit;
  • we follow data minimisation: only anonymous events about gameplay progress are reported, never text you type or other content on your device;
  • the App runs no server of its own and has no account system, so there is no centralised save data to breach, which minimises retention;
  • we use officially distributed, up-to-date SDK versions and run regular security self-checks.

Despite these measures, please understand that the internet is never absolutely secure. Due to technical limits and possible malicious attacks, we cannot guarantee perfect security at all times. If a personal information security incident occurs, we will activate our contingency plan, take remedial measures, inform you of the incident, its likely impact and the steps you can take (through in-app announcements or email), and report to regulators as required by law.

10. Children and minors

The App simulates a life using real statistics and deals with death, illness, poverty, marriage and childbirth. It suits teenagers and adults who can engage with those themes. We do not market the App to children under 14 and do not knowingly collect their personal information.

  • If you are under 14, please read this policy with a parent or guardian and use the App only with their consent;
  • If you are under 18, we recommend using the App with guidance from a guardian, who can help you understand both this policy and the game's content;
  • Where we confirm that information belongs to a child, we process it only with the guardian's separate consent and apply stricter safeguards;
  • If a guardian believes we have collected a child's information without consent, please email us (section 14) and we will delete it promptly after verification.

11. Ads, cross-promotion and opt-outs

11.1 Current status of ads: the Android build includes Google AdMob capability, but advertising is switched off in the live configuration, so you will normally see no ads in the current version. If we enable ads later, they will be app-open ads and an interstitial after a life ends; we will update this policy and notify you prominently in the App at that time.

Once ads are enabled, Google AdMob may personalise them using your device identifier and coarse region. You can turn off ad personalisation:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalisation";
  • Browser (web build): clear and block third-party cookies in your browser settings, or manage Google ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com;
  • Content blockers: on the web build you can use an ad-blocking extension to limit ad scripts; core gameplay is unaffected.

After opting out you may still see ads, but they will be less relevant and may appear more often. Opting out does not affect the basic processing needed to serve and measure ads.

11.2 Cross-promotion

The App may show icons and links for other games by the same developer (for example Cookie Factory or Line Go) on the home, loading or end-of-life screens. These entries are rendered by the in-app promotion module from remote configuration; they do not read your personal information and do not share it with the promoted games. Tapping one takes you to an external page such as itch.io, where cookies and visits are handled by that platform under its own privacy policy. If you prefer not to see promotions, simply ignore or dismiss them; gameplay is unaffected.

12. Local storage and similar technologies

The App does not use browser cookies for cross-site tracking. To save progress and speed up loading, we use local storage on your device:

  • Local saves: current life year, the four stats (health, mood, knowledge, social), job and income, family information, life archive, achievements and favourites;
  • Preferences: speed setting, auto-play pace, layout mode (auto / web / mobile) and language;
  • Configuration cache: cached remote configuration and announcements to avoid repeated downloads (plus asset caching on the web build);
  • Analytics identifier: a random anonymous ID the analytics service uses to distinguish devices.

You can delete this local information by clearing app data or browser data. Once deleted, game progress and past lives cannot be recovered.

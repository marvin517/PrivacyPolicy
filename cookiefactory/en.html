Introduction

Cookie Factory (the "App") is an idle clicker game: tap cookies to earn production, unlock and upgrade facilities, earn achievements and prestige. The App is independently developed and operated by the developer Marvin ("we", "us"), and is currently provided as an Android app (distributed via Google Play) and a web version (played in the browser via itch.io and similar platforms).

We take the protection of your personal information seriously. This Policy explains what information we collect, why, how we use and store it, with whom we share it, and what rights you have. We process personal information based on the principles of legitimacy, purpose limitation, data minimization and security.

This Policy in three sentences ① The App requires no account to play — your game progress is stored only on your device;
② We do not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS or call-log permissions, and we do not collect precise location or biometric data;
③ The App includes advertising and analytics services (AdMob / GameAnalytics) that collect necessary information such as device identifiers; you can disable personalized ads in your system settings.

By using the App you acknowledge that you have read and agreed to this Policy. Where separate consent is required by law, we will present a prominent notice and obtain your explicit consent before processing.

1. Scope of this Policy

1.1 Products covered: This Policy applies to the Android version of Cookie Factory (distributed via Google Play) and the web version (played in the browser via itch.io and similar platforms). Features may differ between channels (for example, in-app purchases and some ad formats are Android-only); the version you actually use prevails.

1.2 Not covered: Distribution platforms hosting the web version (such as itch.io) may themselves use cookies and analytics; their data processing is governed by their own privacy policies, not this Policy. Third-party ads displayed within the App and third-party pages you navigate to are provided by the respective service providers, who are independently responsible for their data practices. We recommend reading their privacy policies before submitting any information to them.

1.3 Data controller: Marvin (independent developer); contact details in Section 14.

If future features require processing any of the above, we will inform you of the purpose, method and scope in advance and obtain your separate consent as required by law before doing so.

4.1 Other sharing scenarios

Beyond the SDK providers above, we do not actively share or transfer your personal information, and we never sell it. We may share necessary information only:

  • with your explicit consent;
  • as required by laws, regulations, or judicial or administrative authorities;
  • to protect vital interests of yours or others where consent cannot be obtained;
  • in a merger, acquisition or asset transfer, in which case we will inform you of the recipient and require it to remain bound by this Policy.

5. How We Use Information

We use your information only for the following purposes and will seek consent again before any use beyond them:

  • Core features: saving and restoring game progress, granting ad rewards and purchased entitlements;
  • Security and fairness: detecting abnormal devices, preventing cheating, maintaining service stability;
  • Product improvement: analyzing progression and churn to optimize difficulty, UI and performance;
  • Advertising and measurement: showing ads, counting impressions and clicks, evaluating performance and controlling frequency;
  • Customer support: responding to inquiries, complaints and rights requests;
  • Legal compliance: cooperating with regulators and responding to lawful requests.

We will not use your personal information for unrelated purposes, for automated decision-making with legal effect on you, or sell or rent it to any third party.

6. Storage and Cross-Border Transfer

6.1 How it is stored

Your game progress and settings are stored locally on your device (browser local storage for the web version; the app's private directory on Android) and are never uploaded to our servers. Analytics data is stored on GameAnalytics' and the ad providers' servers.

6.3 Location and cross-border transfer

Please note: Google AdMob, Google Play Billing and GameAnalytics are provided by entities outside your country. To enable advertising, purchase verification and analytics, relevant device identifiers, IP addresses and usage data may be transferred to and processed in foreign countries (including the United States and Denmark). Such transfers are necessary for the service, and we require the recipients to maintain protection standards no lower than this Policy and applicable law.

Beyond the above, we do not transfer your personal information abroad. If new cross-border scenarios arise, we will inform you, conduct a personal information protection impact assessment, and obtain your separate consent as required by law.

6.4 If the service ends

If the App ceases operation, we will notify you in advance via in-game notices and the store listing, and delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period unless the law requires otherwise.

7. Sharing, Transfer and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. The processing and sharing list is in Section 4. In addition, as permitted by laws and regulations, we may share, transfer or disclose personal information without prior consent in these situations:

  • directly related to national security or defense;
  • directly related to public safety, public health or major public interests;
  • directly related to criminal investigation, prosecution, trial or judgment enforcement;
  • to protect the vital interests of you or another person where consent is hard to obtain;
  • personal information you have made public yourself;
  • information collected from lawfully public sources, such as news reports or government disclosures.

8. Information Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your information:

  • HTTPS / TLS encryption in transit to prevent interception or tampering;
  • a least-privilege approach granting only the access necessary for each feature;
  • no self-operated servers and no server-side account system, minimizing data retention;
  • officially released, promptly updated SDK versions and periodic security reviews.

Despite these measures, no internet environment is absolutely secure. In the event of a personal information security incident, we will activate our response plan, take remedial action, and notify you of the incident, its possible impact and suggested precautions via push or announcement, and report to regulators as required.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is a casual idle game suitable for general audiences, but we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.

  • If you are under 14, please read this Policy with a parent or guardian and use the App only with their consent;
  • If you are under 18, we recommend using the App under guardian guidance;
  • For confirmed children's personal information, we process it only with the guardian's separate consent and with stricter safeguards;
  • If a guardian finds we have collected a child's information without consent, contact us per Section 14 and we will delete it promptly after verification.

11. Personalized Ads and Opt-Out

Ads shown in the App may be personalized by third-party ad platforms (such as Google AdMob) based on your device identifiers and coarse location. You can disable personalized ads:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization";
  • Browser (web): clear and block third-party cookies in browser settings, or manage Google ad personalization at adssettings.google.com;
  • Content blockers: the web version works with ad-blocking extensions; this does not affect core gameplay.

After opting out you may still see non-personalized ads, possibly with higher frequency. Opting out does not affect the basic data processing required for ad delivery and measurement.

12. Local Storage

The App does not use browser cookies for cross-site tracking. To save progress and speed up loading, we use local storage on your device, including:

  • Local saves: cookie count, facility progress, achievements, prestige records, buff states and settings;
  • Cache: cached images, audio and configuration to avoid repeat downloads (web only);
  • SDK local identifiers: local identifiers created by ad and analytics SDKs to distinguish devices.

You can delete this local information by clearing app or browser data. After deletion, game progress cannot be recovered.

Introduction

Huehive (the "Game") is a casual puzzle: tap the color chips at the bottom to queue orders, and worker ants carry colored grains out of the nest one by one, filling in the pixel painting — completing the picture clears the level. There are only 1–3 stars and coin rewards, no fail states. The Game is developed and published by independent developer Marvin ("we", "us"). It is currently available as an Android build (distributed via Google Play) and a web build (running in the browser via itch.io and similar platforms), in Simplified Chinese and English.

This policy explains what the Game processes, why, and what choices you have. The short version: the Game has no accounts and collects no personal data — your progress exists only on your own device.

The policy in three lines ① No account system and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
② Save data (level progress and stars, coins, upgrade levels, settings and sound preferences) stays in local storage on your device. We never upload it and cannot read it.
③ The current version contains no ads, no in-app purchases and no third-party analytics. The only network requests fetch remote configuration and content updates from a CDN.

1. Scope of this policy

1.1 Products covered: this policy covers the Android version of Huehive (distributed via Google Play) and the web version (running in the browser via itch.io and similar platforms). Features may differ between channels; the version you actually use governs.

1.2 Not covered: the account, payment, analytics and logging practices of distribution platforms (such as itch.io and Google Play) are governed by those platforms' own privacy policies and are not covered by this policy.

1.3 Data controller: Marvin (independent developer). Contact details are in section 9.

2. What we do not collect

  • There is no account system and no sign-in.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • We do not read or upload your save data: level progress and stars, coins, upgrade levels (ant count / speed / queue slots / coin gain), language and sound preferences stay in local storage on your device (the app's private data directory on Android, browser local storage on the web build). Uninstalling the app or clearing browser data deletes them.
  • We do not read other apps on your device and do not collect advertising IDs.

2.2 If you email us

If you choose to contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to answer your question. It is not used for anything else and never added to a marketing list. Beyond that, the Game has no input fields of any kind.

3. What happens when you are online

  • The Game is fully playable offline and does not require a connection.
  • On launch the Game requests a public remote configuration file from a CDN (jsDelivr) used for versioning and parameter delivery; when a newer version is detected, content update resources (such as level and text adjustments) are downloaded as needed. These requests carry no account, save data or device identifier; the CDN provider records ordinary request logs (such as IP address, time and User-Agent) under its own policy.
  • The current version contains no ads, no in-app purchases and no third-party analytics.

The requests exist only to fetch public configuration and updated content. We do not read, receive or store the CDN's access logs. If they fail (offline, blocked, or otherwise), the Game falls back to content already cached on your device or built into it, and plays normally.

5. Your choices

  • Delete your save: use "Reset progress" in the Game, or uninstall the app / clear browser data.
  • Block the network: disconnecting does not affect play; the Game uses content already cached or built in.
  • EEA / UK / California residents: because the Game collects no personal data there is normally nothing to access or delete; contact us below with any question.

Exercising these choices never restricts a feature or degrades the experience — that data was never in our hands to begin with.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. Since the Game collects no personal data at all, no children's data is retained either. If a parent or guardian believes we hold any such information, please email us using section 9 and we will address it promptly after verification.

7. Security and retention

  • The Game has no accounts and no server that stores personal data; there is no database containing your information.
  • Save data lives only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear browser data. We do not back it up, export or copy it.
  • Configuration and update delivery uses HTTPS, so content cannot be modified in transit.
  • Email correspondence is kept only until your question is resolved; you may ask us to delete it at any time.

The reasoning is simple: what is never collected needs no protection. That is our default position on data.

8. Changes

If a future version adds ads (such as Google AdMob), in-app purchases, accounts or anonymous analytics, this policy will be updated before that version ships and noted on the store page. Updates are published on this page with a new version number, update date and effective date; material changes to purpose, method or categories of data will be highlighted prominently.
